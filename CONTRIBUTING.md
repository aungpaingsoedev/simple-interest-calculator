# Contributing

All contributions, bug reports, bug fixes, documentation improvements, enhancements, and ideas are welcome.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue to report a bug or suggest a change, or fork the repository and open a pull request.
2. Keep changes small and focused on the simple interest calculator.
3. Check your work by opening `index.html` in a browser. The formula is `SI = (P × R × T) / 100`, and the total amount is `P + SI`.

Please follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
